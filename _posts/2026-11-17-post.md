Hong L, Chen G, Liu Y, Cao Y and Li X et al. evolved the AAV6 capsid in primary human T cells and identified AAV.APR31, a variant with an APR motif that engages CD7 and transduces T and NK cells at nearly 100–1,000-fold lower doses than AAV6. This capsid supports transient delivery, site-specific knock-in and transposon integration, and can generate CAR-T cells in vivo.

They selected the library in primary T cells and recovered a family of variants with an APR motif in variable region IV; the lead, AAV.APR31, carries the 454-AAPRANE-460 insertion. CRISPR screens and follow-up work support a layered-entry model: AAV.APR31 still uses the canonical AAVR pathway, while CD7 acts as a dominant, lineage-restricted enhancer on T and NK cells. AlphaFold 3 modeling suggested that the APR motif forms a contact interface with the CD7 extracellular domain that is not seen with AAV6. Under matched conditions, AAV.APR31 outperformed the T cell-tropic capsid AAV-hT7 and supported CAR, NY-ESO-1 TCR and CAR-NK knock-in, as well as Sleeping Beauty transposon delivery, at much lower MOIs. When CAR-positive numbers were matched, the resulting CAR-T cells were functionally comparable to lentiviral products and cleared SK-OV-3 xenografts. Systemic delivery of AAV.APR31 encoding a CD22-CAR or MSLN-CAR, after adoptive transfer of human T cells, also generated CAR-T cells in vivo that controlled NALM-6 leukemia and AsPC-1 pancreatic tumours, unlike AAV6. The evolved capsid showed reduced liver and lung transduction relative to AAV6, although residual AAVR-dependent entry means this is preferential tropism rather than absolute tissue specificity.

<h3>Questions to be addressed</h3>
1. How durable and safe is AAV.APR31-mediated in vivo CAR-T generation in immunocompetent or more fully humanized models, given residual AAVR-dependent transduction of non-lymphoid tissues?
2. Will pre-existing anti-AAV immunity, or variable CD7 expression, limit clinical transduction, and can the capsid be further evolved for neutralizing-antibody evasion?
3. How does AAV.APR31 compare head-to-head in vivo with other in vivo T-cell delivery platforms, including AAV-hT7, lipid nanoparticles and lentiviral or enveloped vectors?
