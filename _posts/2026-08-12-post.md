Piraner DI and Abedi MH et al. modified the SNIPRs to detect soluble ligands like TGF-β and VEGF to activate CAR-T cells in the tumor microenvironment. Such an approach enhances the safety of CAR-T cell therapeutics. The SNIPRs lack an LNR domain, which is critical to the activation of the notch receptor via a force-induced mechanism, indicating they adapt an alternative pathway for receptor activation.
